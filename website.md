The overall brand architecture

I think Shashwatamuda can be developed around one central idea:

Ancient Wisdom. Contemporary Well-being. Eternal Joy.

And underneath it:

YOGA → Body • Breath • Mind
MUSIC → Nāda • Rāga • Harmony
JÑĀNA → Knowledge • Tradition • Wisdom
RESEARCH → Evidence • Innovation • Integration
SEVA → Youth • Community • Social Well-being

This gives you room to grow Shashwatamuda into a serious wellness, education, research and cultural platform without restricting it to only yoga classes.
