## Where we work

- **Schools** — wellness weeks, yoga and music sessions, value education
- **Colleges** — stress and focus programs, International Yoga Day
- **Communities** — open-air practice, cultural and music programs
- **Yoga camps** — day and residential camps for institutions
- **Youth programs** — leadership, personality development and volunteering
- **Rehabilitation settings and special populations** — adapted, gentle practice in partnership with care providers
