## Our approach

Our yoga teaching draws on the classical Hatha and Raja Yoga texts, taught in a way that is safe, progressive and relevant to modern life. Whether you come for fitness, calm, recovery or deeper practice, you will start with a short orientation so we can place you in the right group.

<Callout title="A note on yoga therapy">
Therapeutic yoga is a complementary, educational practice. It supports, but does not replace, medical diagnosis and treatment. We ask every participant to keep their healthcare provider informed.
</Callout>

## What a regular batch looks like

1. **Centering and breath awareness** for the first few minutes.
2. **Joint mobility and sun salutations** to warm the body.
3. **Asana sequence** adapted to the week's theme.
4. **Pranayama** appropriate to the group's experience.
5. **Deep relaxation** or a short meditation to close.
