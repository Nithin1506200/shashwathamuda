At six in the morning on 21 June, more than two hundred people rolled out mats on damp grass in a neighbourhood park. Here is what we learnt.

## 1. Keep the protocol simple

We used the Common Yoga Protocol, which is designed for mixed groups. Clear cues, visible demonstrators at four corners, and a microphone that actually worked.

## 2. Let young people lead

Our youth volunteers handled registration, mat placement and the warm-up. Giving them ownership changed the energy of the morning.

## 3. End with music

A short Carnatic interlude on the Veena after the final relaxation turned a fitness event into something people lingered for.
