## Research

Our research work sits at the meeting point of classical Yoga and contemporary integrative medicine. Current and recent areas include:

- Evidence-informed yoga modules for stress, sleep and musculoskeletal health
- Yoga therapy protocols for lifestyle conditions
- Music and raga-based relaxation as an adjunct to yoga practice
- Yoga in schools and youth well-being

**Publications and collaborations** will be listed here as they become available. Researchers and clinicians interested in joint work are welcome to [collaborate with us](/collaborate).
