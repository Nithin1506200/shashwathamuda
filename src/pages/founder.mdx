## Journey

Nikhil Srinivas grew up with two disciplines side by side: yoga and Carnatic music. Formal yoga education and teaching led to doctoral research in Integrative Medicine (Yoga), while years of Veena study shaped a conviction that music and yoga are two doors to the same room.

Shashwatamuda grew out of this. It is an attempt to teach both with authenticity, to study them honestly, and to carry them into schools, communities and young people's lives.

## Areas of work

- Yoga education and practice
- PhD research in Integrative Medicine (Yoga)
- Yoga therapy and research
- Carnatic music and Veena
- Community programs
- Youth leadership
- National and international experiences in yoga and music

## Philosophy

> The body is the first instrument, the breath the second, and sound the third. Tune them together and joy is not something you chase. It is what remains.
