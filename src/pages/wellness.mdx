## Offerings

- **Individual consultations.** A conversation, a gentle assessment and a personalised practice plan, reviewed every few weeks.
- **Structured wellness programs.** Six to twelve week programs for sleep, stress, back care and lifestyle.
- **Online sessions.** Live guided practice for those who cannot come to the centre.
- **Institutional programs.** Designed for corporates, hospitals, schools and wellness centres.
- **Retreats.** Weekend and residential retreats combining yoga, music and quiet.
- **Evidence-informed Yoga interventions.** Protocol-based modules developed with our research advisors.

<Callout title="Important">
Shashwatamuda's wellness and educational services are complementary practices. They are not medical treatment and do not replace the advice of a qualified healthcare professional. Programs described for specific conditions are designed to support, not substitute for, medical care.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. Write to us with a few lines about what you are looking for.
2. We schedule an initial consultation, at the centre or online.
3. You receive a practice plan and a schedule of follow-ups.
