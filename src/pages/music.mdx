## Music as a path

In Indian thought, *Nāda* is the subtle vibration underlying both the outer world and the inner. Learning Carnatic music, and especially the Veena, is a way of listening to that vibration closely. Our music programs welcome complete beginners and serious students alike.

A guided listening and breathing session built around a single raga, played live on the Veena. No musical training is needed. These sessions are open to the public and are also offered to institutions.

## Learning the Veena

Beginners start with the foundational exercises (sarali and janta varisai), move to geethams and varnams, and then to kritis. Practice instruments are available at the centre for the first weeks; we help students source their own Veena when they are ready.
